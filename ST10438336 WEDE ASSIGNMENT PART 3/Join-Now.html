Is there really no contract?

Correct. All memberships are month to month. You can cancel from your dashboard with thirty days notice and there is no cancellation fee.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes. Standard and Premium members can freeze for up to three months per year at no cost. Student members can freeze for up to two months.

Can I train at any club?

Standard and Premium members can train at all twenty branches nationwide. Student members have full access to their chosen home club.

What do I need to bring on my first visit?

Bring your digital access card on your phone and a towel. Comfortable training clothes and closed shoes are recommended.

How do I cancel?

Log into your member dashboard, open the account section and select Cancel Membership. No phone calls or paperwork required.
