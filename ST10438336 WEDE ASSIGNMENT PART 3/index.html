Welcome to The Gym Company

We removed the barriers between you and your goals.

Founded in 1997 by brothers Erik and Sarel du Toit, The Gym Company was the first to bring the low-cost, high-value fitness concept to South Africa. What began as a single gym in Fourways, Johannesburg, is now a nationwide family of 20 clubs.

Every club is equipped with quality strength and cardio equipment, clean changing rooms and friendly staff. No sales pressure, no complicated contracts and no surprises on your bank statement.
