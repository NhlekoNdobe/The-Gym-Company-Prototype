About Us

Twenty-eight years of accessible fitness.

From one gym in Fourways to a nationwide community of twenty clubs.

Our story

Founded on a simple idea.

In 1997, brothers Erik and Sarel du Toit opened the first Gym Company in Fourways, Johannesburg. At the time, gym memberships in South Africa were expensive, tied to long contracts and designed for a narrow slice of the market. The brothers saw a different opportunity: build a gym that stripped away the extras, kept the essentials, and priced it so that ordinary South Africans could afford to train.

That idea worked. Over the following decades The Gym Company expanded steadily, opening clubs in shopping centres, industrial nodes and residential suburbs across the country. Today there are twenty branches from Gauteng to KwaZulu-Natal and the Western Cape, and the company remains independently owned and run by the founding family.

Our journey

Milestones along the way

Erik and Sarel du Toit open the first Gym Company in Fourways, Johannesburg, introducing the low-cost, high-value model to South Africa.

The company opens its first clubs outside Gauteng, bringing affordable fitness to new provinces.

Member access cards are introduced, allowing members to train at any hour without staffed reception.

The Gym Company expands into the Western Cape, opening branches in Cape Town CBD, Bellville and Somerset West.

The network reaches twenty clubs across four provinces, serving thousands of members every day.

Mission

What we do every day

To make health and fitness accessible to every South African by providing affordable, safe and well-equipped gyms without hidden costs.

Vision

Where we are going

To be the largest and most inclusive fitness community in Africa, where everyone, regardless of budget, can achieve their wellness goals.

What we stand for

Our values

Four principles guide how we build clubs, hire staff and price memberships.

Accessibility

Fitness should not depend on your budget. We keep our prices low and our doors open to everyone.

Safety

Clean equipment, well-lit clubs, and staff on hand. Members should feel secure at any hour.

Honesty

No hidden fees, no lock-in traps, no fine print that works against our members. What you see is what you pay.

Community

Our clubs are places where strangers become training partners and members stay for years.

Our people

Led by the family that started it.

Erik and Sarel du Toit still run The Gym Company today. They work with a small head office team in Fourways and more than three hundred club staff across the country, from receptionists and cleaners to personal trainers and club managers.

Every club manager has come up through the business, which means the person running your local gym understands what members actually need.
